# Audience — k-sebe-yoga

Who the landing page speaks to, their needs, and their context.

## Primary audience

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Demographics** | Russian-speaking women, 25–50 years old |
| **Location** | Potsdam / Berlin, Germany |
| **Language** | Russian (native) |
| **Life stage** | Settled in Germany (immigrants, expat partners, long-term residents); may work in tech, creative, or care professions; have established daily routines |

## Psychographics

- **Seeking depth, not fitness.** They've tried (or are tired of) high-intensity workouts, gym culture, and performance-oriented sports. What they want is a practice that feels like coming home to themselves.
- **Urban fatigue.** Berlin/Potsdam fast pace, digital noise, always-on culture. They crave slowness, presence, real connection.
- **Women's space.** A preference for a safe, women-only environment where they can drop the social mask — no performance pressure, no judgment, no comparison.
- **Spiritual but not religious.** Open to inner work, intuition, energy, somatic practices — but sceptical of dogma, excessive mysticism, or commercialised "wellness."
- **Value-conscious.** Free offering attracts those who prioritise genuine connection over paid services. Paying for a yoga class is common; choosing a free one signals a desire for community over transaction.

## Pains

| Pain | How k-sebe-yoga addresses it |
|------|------------------------------|
| Loss of self in daily routine (work, family, migration stress) | Practice framed as "returning to yourself" — a deliberate pause to reconnect with feelings, desires, intuition |
| Isolation in a new country | Free outdoor group creates low-barrier community; shared language and cultural background |
| Body image / performance anxiety | Explicitly "not about how flexible you are" — no skill requirement, no comparison |
| Decision fatigue | Simple offering: one day, one time, one place. No sign-up forms, no subscriptions — just show up |
| Lack of meaningful women's circles | Small group, intimate setting, themed practices (desires, self-worth, feminine energy, creativity) |

## Audience signals (what they respond to)

- **Warm, personal tone** — not corporate, not guru-like. A real person inviting other real people.
- **Permission to be imperfect** — "come as you are" is the core emotional value.
- **Early morning framing** — "before the city wakes up" positions the practice as a sacred/private time, not a chore.
- **Free + outdoor** — removes financial and spatial barriers; signals trust and generosity.

## Secondary audience

- Partners / friends of primary audience who share the invitation
- Russian-speaking wellness communities in Berlin/Potsdam (Telegram channels, expat groups)
- Women visiting Berlin/Potsdam who want a one-off meaningful experience

## Not for

- People seeking fitness tracking / measurable progress
- Yoga practitioners looking for advanced asana or alignment correction
- Men (the practice is explicitly women-only)
- German-speaking audience (the landing page is Russian-only by design)
