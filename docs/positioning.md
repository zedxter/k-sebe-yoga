# Positioning — k-sebe-yoga

How k-sebe-yoga positions itself in the Berlin/Potsdam wellness landscape.

## One-liner

**Бесплатная уличная йога для женщин в Потсдаме — не про тело, а про возвращение к себе.**  
*(Free outdoor yoga for women in Potsdam — not about the body, about returning to yourself.)*

## Category

k-sebe-yoga does not compete in the "yoga studio" or "fitness" category. It belongs to a different category:

> **Women's community practice** — a low-barrier, donation-free gathering that uses yoga as a vehicle for inner work, not as a physical discipline.

## Positioning statement

> For Russian-speaking women in Potsdam/Berlin who feel disconnected from themselves in the rush of daily life, k-sebe-yoga is a free outdoor practice that offers a space to return to your feelings, desires, and intuition — without performance pressure, without membership fees, without needing to know any poses.

## Competitive landscape

| Competitor type | What they offer | How we differ |
|-----------------|----------------|---------------|
| **Yoga studios** (Berlin/Potsdam) | Paid classes, fixed schedule, indoor, mixed-gender, teacher-led | Free, outdoor, women-only, community-driven, not skill-based |
| **Free park yoga** (meetup groups) | Often donation-based, mixed, English/German | Russian-language, explicitly women-only, themed around inner work |
| **Wellness apps** (Headspace, etc.) | Digital, individual, subscription | Physical, group, free, real-world connection |
| **Women's circles / cacao ceremonies** | Paid, often in English, spiritual | Free, Russian-speaking, outdoors, accessible, no spiritual obligation |
| **Fitness / CrossFit / run clubs** | Performance-oriented, mixed, membership | Soft, gentle, women-only, not about metrics |

## Key differentiators

| Differentiator | Why it matters |
|----------------|----------------|
| **Free** | Removes financial barrier; signals that the practice is about genuine connection, not monetisation |
| **Russian-language** | Serves a specific immigrant/expat community that may not feel at home in English/German wellness spaces |
| **Women-only** | Creates psychological safety; participants can be vulnerable without performance or social pressure |
| **Outdoor (Ruinenberg)** | Nature as co-therapist; free from studio walls; beautiful setting that adds to the experience |
| **Early morning (7:00)** | Positions the practice as intentional self-care before the day begins; creates a "sacred time" ritual |
| **Themed practices** | Each session explores a topic (desires, self-worth, feminine energy, creativity) — gives depth beyond asana |
| **No skill requirement** | "Come as you are" — radically inclusive for women who have never done yoga or feel intimidated by studios |

| Energy | Calm, inviting, not pushy or salesy |
| Visuals | Soft earth tones, natural light, women in comfortable clothes, nature setting |

## Communication principles

1. **Lead with the feeling, not the activity.** "Return to yourself" before "yoga."
2. **Remove every barrier.** Free, no registration, no special skills, no gear except a mat.
3. **Be specific.** Date, time, exact location, duration — clarity builds trust.
4. **Don't oversell.** The invitation is enough. Let the experience speak for itself.
5. **Stay in your lane.** This is not about weight loss, flexibility, or stress reduction as a metric. It's about inner connection.
